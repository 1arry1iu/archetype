**Conceptual definition:** Archetype = a theoretically situated, recurrently recognizable, structurally organized pattern or pattern-generating specification whose manifestations, interpretations, and proposed mechanisms must be distinguished and evaluated independently.

**Ontology:** Archetypal Concept × Theoretical Framework × Structural Invariants × Variable Properties × Manifestations × Contexts × Interpretations × Proposed Mechanisms × Evidence × Transformations.

**Analytical process:** Phenomenon Identification → Contextualization → Pattern Detection → Structural Abstraction → Conceptual Differentiation → Comparative Analysis → Explanatory Hypothesis → Evidence Evaluation → Formal Representation → Validation → Revision.

**Generative process:** Structural Configuration → Contextual Activation → Cognitive and Affective Mediation → Motivational Organization → Relational Interaction → Symbolic or Behavioral Expression → Consequence → Feedback → Transformation.

**Epistemological principle:** Distinguish observed recurrences, interpretive constructions, historical theories, empirically supported mechanisms, computational models, and speculative hypotheses.

**Methodological principle:** Never infer an inherited universal psychological structure merely from recurrent symbolic figures, narrative configurations, or cultural practices.

**Ontological principle:** Do not presume one archetype ontology applies unqualified across psychology, mythology, religion, literature, anthropology, cognition, and information engineering.

**Scientific principle:** Specify referent, structure, applicability, evidence, alternative explanations, uncertainty, and revision criteria.

**Information principle:** Organize archetypes as provenance-aware networks of concepts, instances, structures, interpretations, theories, hypotheses, and evidence.

**Generative principle:** Specify structural constraints and conditional mechanisms rather than prescribing uniform surface manifestations.

**Normative principle:** Protect individual agency, cultural specificity, interpretive plurality, informed consent, and legitimate authority.

**Research objective:** Discover which patterns recur, where, why, through which mechanisms, with what meanings, and under which boundary conditions.

**Design objective:** Build precise, interoperable, culturally situated, structurally generative, empirically accountable, evolving archetype models.

**Frontier:** Archetype as Label → Symbolic Configuration → Relational Structure → Contextual Process → Testable Hypothesis → Generative Model → Governed Knowledge Network.
