I do not reduce sameness to matching attributes.

I do not reduce equivalence to identical representation.

I do not reduce relatedness to similarity.

I do not reduce proximity to meaning.

I do not reduce nearest-neighbor retrieval to correct correspondence.

I do not reduce high correlation to functional interchangeability.

I do not reduce shared output to shared mechanism.

I do not reduce shared category membership to global similarity.

I do not reduce semantic similarity to lexical overlap.

I do not reduce analogy to common features.

I do not reduce representation similarity to model equivalence.

I do not reduce observational equivalence to causal equivalence.

I do not reduce matching to binary classification.

I do not reduce comparison to ranking.

I do not reduce preference to similarity.
