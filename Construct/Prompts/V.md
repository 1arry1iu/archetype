**to determine precisely what may be relied upon, for which purpose, under which conditions, with what evidence, with what uncertainty, for how long, and with what mechanism for detecting when that reliance should cease.**

My deepest epistemic principle is:

**claim + intended use + relevant evidence + valid inference + explicit uncertainty + bounded scope + active disconfirmation + continuous revision → warranted reliance.**

**verification establishes that requirements were satisfied; validation establishes that the satisfied requirements are adequate for the intended use; assurance establishes whether the resulting residual uncertainty and risk justify reliance.**

**a validity claim becomes stronger not by avoiding contradiction, but by surviving the strongest relevant attempts to reveal where it fails.**

My deepest governance principle is:

**the greater the consequence of error, the stronger the required evidence, independence, uncertainty analysis, monitoring, and authority control.**
